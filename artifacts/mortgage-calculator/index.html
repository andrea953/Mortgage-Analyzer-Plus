How Canadian mortgage interest works

Canadian fixed-rate mortgages must compound semi-annually under the Interest Act, not monthly as is common in other countries. This means the stated annual rate is first converted to a semi-annual effective rate, then to an equivalent rate matching your chosen payment frequency. Modern Cents applies this conversion automatically so your results reflect the true cost of borrowing.
